# Dino Lab 0.1.22

- Added a dedicated **Combat Arena** main tab, replacing the Attacks tab in species profiles.
- Added continuously animated clashing swords to the sidebar, with vibrant blades and impact sparks.
- Added two animated portraits using the same artwork as the species library. Each changes with its selected dinosaur and has independent growth, Prime/Frail and HP controls below it.
- Preserved attack choices, mutation bonuses and resistance, entombment levels, stacking, bleeding, Hypermetabolic scenarios and two-way damage/hit counts.
- Applied the recorded health penalty to both combatants: full damage at 50% HP and above, scaling to half damage at 10% HP, with no further reduction below 10%.
- Supported all 22 playable dinosaurs and eight adult wildlife entries, including Hypsilophodon's existing Source 2 bite/HP profile. Saved attack overrides apply independently to both sides.
- Removed the general stacking and calculation explanation paragraphs from the bottom of Combat Arena, together with the earlier attack-reference panels and primary-damage notice. Combat controls and calculations are unchanged by this cleanup.
