# Dino Lab 0.1.7

- Simplified the Mutations tab to its 41 active entries. Removed the details, clarification and limitation panels, uncertainty and cross-check badges, top reference notice, and historical mutations section and counter.
- Replaced the generic Diet filter and dropdown with Universal, Carnivore and Herbivore groups. All 41 active entries remain available; unlockable entries sit in their applicable group with their unlock conditions.
- Removed Tactile Endurance from the attack calculator; it remains in the mutation reference as a stamina effect.
- Made all three active damage boosters visible in both combatant panels: Accelerated Prey Drive, Hemomania and Hypermetabolic Inanition. Carnivore-only eligibility remains enforced, with a clear reason for unavailable choices. Each side's eligible selections affect its own outgoing attack.
- Aligned Bite force with the other statistics table headings.

Existing strengths, unlock conditions, eligibility restrictions and original source records are preserved. No mutation percentages or hit-count formulas changed.
