⚙ Edit data
What are mechanics? Some abilities in The Isle only work when the target is light enough relative to you — grabbing, pinning, knocking down. These thresholds show as shaded bands on the Weight chart so you can see which species you can affect.

Each mechanic is drawn on one chart. Threshold mode shades everything at or below your stat multiplied by the value — 0.50 on the Weight chart means the target must weigh half your weight or less. Multiplier mode draws a single line at your stat multiplied by the value — 2.0 on the Bite Force chart means double your bite force.

Your edits are personal. They only change what you see, in this browser. Anything you leave untouched keeps following the shared sheet, so updates still reach you. Edited rows are marked with an amber edge and a ↺ button to revert; rows you added have a cyan edge.
