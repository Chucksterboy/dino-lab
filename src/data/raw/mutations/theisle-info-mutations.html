Mutations explained

Last updated: May 28, 2026

Mutations are passive abilities that change how your dinosaur survives, fights, or feeds. Every species has a pool of mutations it can roll — the right picks let a smaller animal punch above its weight; a bad roll can leave you with a useless slot. This is the full reference: every mutation in The Isle: Evrima with its value, restrictions, and (where applicable) how to unlock it.

How mutations work

Mutations slot into your dinosaur's active loadout and apply their effects passively for as long as they remain equipped. There are three broad categories:

  • • Lifecycle mutations — the default pool available to (most) species.
  • • Slot 2 exclusives — only roll on the second mutation slot.
  • • Unlockable lifestyle mutations — gated by in-game tasks (e.g. kill 5 players at night, jump 50 times, drink saltwater until dehydrated). Once unlocked, they appear in your pool for the specified slot.

Tags below mark restrictions like Carnivore, Herbivore, Female Only, or Group Leader Only. Values are the listed in-game magnitudes for the effect.

Unlockable lifestyle mutations

Gated by in-game tasks — once unlocked, they appear in your pool.

Augmented Tapetum

Carnivore

Increased vision at night.

How: Kill 5 players at night. Unlocks on Slot 2.

Enhanced Digestion

Decrease nutrition decay rate.

How: Have nutrients for 60 minutes. Unlocks on Slot 2 / 3.

Heightened Ghrelin

Increased overeating capacity by a large amount.

How: Maintain current hunger above 80% for 30 minutes. Unlocks on Slot 2.

Multichambered Lungs

Increases stamina regeneration threshold.

How: Drain 4,500 stamina by sprinting or fast-swimming. Unlocks on Slot 2 / 3.

Osteophagic

Carnivore

Able to consume bones to regenerate fractures faster.

How: Unlock by eating bones while you have a broken bone.

Parthenogenesis

Female Only

Lets you nest without a mate. Mutation is not passed on to children.

How: Unlocks on Slot 2.

Prolific Reproduction

Female Only

Your babies have increased health and stamina regen. They need less food and grow faster.

How: Unlocks on Slot 2.

Reinforced Tendons

Jumping costs less stamina. Reduces takeoff stamina for Pteranodon.

How: Unlock by jumping 50 times.

Reniculate Kidneys

Can drink saltwater. Thirst pool: 1,000. Thirst intake value while drinking saltwater: −2.5%.

How: Lose 1,250 thirst by drinking saltwater (50 thirst grants the 'Fluid Deficient' debuff; 1,200 thirst removes it). Unlocks on Slot 2 / 3.
