ROLE & DECISION LOOP

Overview and gameplay loop

Mobile bruiser

Rotate attackers. Keep the hunt moving.

Allosaurus suits players who enjoy measured close-range pressure and teamwork. Use the forest edge to close the gap, commit to a short pounce, then step away to recover stamina. When the target turns, rotate the attacker instead of sending the whole pack in at once.

IDENTIFYING AI PREY IN THE DIET

AI spawns by region and player/server density; spawn descriptions refer to habitat types, not guaranteed coordinates.

Deer

Brown body, long legs and antlers; larger than a goat and often runs in long bursts.

Land AI favoring forest edges, grassland and active player corridors; it has no guaranteed fixed coordinate.
Boar

Low, thick and dark brown; it charges straight when startled and disappears easily in bushes.

Land AI found in forest, forest edge and grassland; density depends on player and server activity.
Goat

Small, pale and compact with short horns; easy to mistake for a light object at range.

Open terrain, hillsides and grassland; its call is often easier to detect before the animal is visible.

PALEONTOLOGY FIELD NOTES

Allosaurus · Science & another perspective

Allosaurus lived during the Late Jurassic, roughly 155–145 million years ago, long before Tyrannosaurus.

Fun fact

Each hand carried three clawed fingers, and air spaces made the skull lighter than its appearance suggests.

Read science and gameplay separately.
